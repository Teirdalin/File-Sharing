# VideoDrop branding policy

The VideoDrop name, logo, and official listing presentation identify Teirdalin's release. No claim of trademark registration is made by this notice.

## Software licenses remain unchanged

Original software code remains under its stated MIT license. FFmpeg and other third-party components retain their own licenses, including the redistribution rights those licenses grant. This policy does not prohibit software resale where a software license permits it, impose extra conditions on GPL-covered code, or revoke rights already granted under another license.

## Brand identity and endorsement

The software licenses do not grant permission to impersonate Teirdalin, claim an unofficial build or listing is the official VideoDrop release, or imply sponsorship, approval, or endorsement that has not been given.

Unofficial and modified releases should clearly identify their publisher and their relationship to the original project. Use a distinct product name and visual identity where needed to avoid confusion with the official release. Do not present copied listing artwork as evidence that Teirdalin published or endorsed your product.

Accurate references to VideoDrop, truthful attribution, identification of genuine software, and uses otherwise permitted by applicable law remain allowed. Any independently licensed material retains the rights granted by its own license.

For permission to use the official identity in a product listing or promotion, contact Teirdalin through [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/teirdalin) or [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/Teirdalin). A donation does not by itself grant branding permission.
